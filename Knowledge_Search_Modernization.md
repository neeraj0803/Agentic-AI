# Project Context Document

## Project Name

Enterprise Knowledge Discovery Enhancement

---

## Background

Employees across Customer Support, Operations, and Engineering teams rely on internal documentation repositories to locate policies, procedures, troubleshooting guides, and technical documentation.

Currently, users access documentation through a legacy keyword-based search experience that often produces irrelevant or incomplete results.

As a result, employees spend significant time manually searching through multiple repositories.

---

## Problem Statement

Employees experience difficulty locating relevant enterprise documents, leading to reduced productivity, delayed issue resolution, and increased dependency on support teams.

The current search experience lacks semantic understanding, resulting in low search relevance and inefficient knowledge discovery.

---

## Supporting Evidence

### User Feedback

- 70% of surveyed employees stated they struggle to find relevant documents.
- Employees reported frequently searching multiple repositories before locating required information.
- Support engineers indicated they often re-answer questions that are already documented.

### Operational Metrics

- Approximately 300 search-related support tickets are raised each month.
- Average document search session duration is 8 minutes.
- Current search success rate is 45%.

### Business Impact

- Time spent searching reduces productive work hours.
- Repeated support requests increase workload on support teams.
- Delays in finding information can impact customer issue resolution timelines.

### Supporting Links

- SharePoint Analytics Dashboard
- Employee Satisfaction Survey FY26
- Service Desk Ticket Report
- Knowledge Portal Usage Report

---

## Business Goal

Improve the enterprise knowledge discovery experience by enabling intelligent document search and retrieval capabilities.

---

## Expected Outcomes

- Reduce average search time by 50%.
- Increase search success rate from 45% to 80%.
- Reduce search-related support tickets by 30%.
- Improve employee satisfaction with the search experience.

---

## Users

### Primary Users

- Employees
- Support Engineers
- Operations Teams

### Secondary Users

- Knowledge Content Owners
- Business Analysts

---

## Known Assumptions

- Enterprise documents already exist within approved repositories.
- Repository access permissions are correctly configured.
- Existing repositories expose required metadata.
- Employees will continue using existing repositories.

---

## Constraints

### Timeline

- Delivery must be completed within 8 weeks.

### Budget

- No additional software licensing costs are approved.

### Technical Constraints

- Solution must integrate with existing repositories.
- Existing security and access control mechanisms must be preserved.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Semantic search
- Document indexing
- Relevance ranking
- Search analytics
- Multi-repository search

### Out of Scope

- Content authoring
- Document lifecycle management
- Knowledge governance redesign
- Repository migration

---

## Risks

- Poor content quality may impact search accuracy.
- Repository metadata may be incomplete.
- Access restrictions could limit indexing coverage.

---

## Additional Notes

The proposed solution should provide measurable improvements without requiring significant changes to existing user workflows.
