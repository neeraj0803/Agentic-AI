# Product Requirements Document

## Problem

Employees cannot find trusted enterprise documents quickly.

## Users

- Employees
- Support Engineers

## Needs

- Find approved knowledge quickly
- Reduce repeat support inquiries

## Expected Impact

- Search time reduced by 50%
- Support productivity improved

## Scope

### In Scope

- Knowledge search
- Search optimization

### Out of Scope

- Workflow automation outside knowledge retrieval

## Assumptions

- Documents already exist in approved repositories.

## Constraints

- No additional licensing costs.
- Delivery within 8 weeks.
